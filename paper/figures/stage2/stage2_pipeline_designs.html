Stage 2 Pipeline — Compact Design Proposals

Goal: reduce white-space, emphasise the novel multi-CNN adapter bank. All designs maintain the same information content as pipeline_v2.png stage 2.

Design A — "Dual-Track Merge" ★ recommended

Two parallel horizontal tracks (H&E encoding on top, TME channel encoding on bottom) converge at the PixCell ControlNet block. The CNN adapter bank is drawn as a single highlighted module with four internal lanes — visually compact yet clearly novel. Loss gradient shown as a single dashed loop.

✓ Strengths

  • ~40% shorter vertically than original
  • CNN adapter bank is a single highlighted block — immediately reads as the novel contribution
  • Two-track layout mirrors the dual-input nature of the model
  • Loss loop is clean single dashed line

✗ Trade-offs

  • Slightly wider than original to fit both tracks side-by-side
  • The "inject" arrow needs a bend

Design B — "Inline Squeeze" minimal

Everything on a single horizontal band. TME channels are tiny icons above the CNN bank, which sits directly below the main data flow. Extremely compact — optimised for a narrow column figure.

✓ Strengths

  • Most compact — fits a single-column journal figure width
  • Main data flow is a clean left→right line
  • CNN labels include their input channel directly — no extra icons

✗ Trade-offs

  • TME channel thumbnails lost — replaced by text labels
  • Less visual distinction between the two input streams

Design C — "Exploded Module" novelty-first

The CNN adapter bank is drawn large and center, with its four lanes expanded vertically. PixCell ControlNet wraps around it as a container. This puts maximum visual emphasis on the novel contribution — the multi-channel CNN adapters — while everything else is compressed to the periphery.

✓ Strengths

  • CNN adapter bank is the largest visual element — maximum emphasis on novelty
  • Shows the adapters inside the PixCell model — architecturally accurate
  • Each CNN lane is labelled with its channel — easy to read
  • Compact left-side TME grid (2×2) saves horizontal space

✗ Trade-offs

  • Taller than Design A/B — needs more vertical space
  • Overlapping boxes may need careful color tuning for print

Design D — "Symmetric Hourglass" balanced

The two inputs (H&E and TME) sit on the left, fan into a central PixCell block, and the output fans out to the right. The CNN adapter bank appears as a distinct coloured sidebar fused to the PixCell block, creating an "hourglass" silhouette that naturally draws the eye to the adapters.

✓ Strengths

  • Balanced L-shaped composition — CNN sidebar naturally draws the eye
  • Two input paths are visually distinct (top = H&E, left-bottom = TME)
  • Compact overall bounding box — works in single or double-column layouts
  • CNN sidebar fused to PixCell shows architectural coupling

✗ Trade-offs

  • Asymmetric — the CNN sidebar hangs below the main flow
  • TME channel thumbnails are small (could add labels)
