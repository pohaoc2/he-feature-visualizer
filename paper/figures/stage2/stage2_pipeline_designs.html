Stage 2 Training Layout Proposals

These mockups now follow the revised PixCell training rule: the mask latent shapes Q tokens inside TME cross-attention but does not bypass to ControlNet. With zero_mask_latent=true, fused = tme(vae_mask) - vae_mask = Σ(Δgroup), and only residuals are sent into the trainable ControlNet.

Design A — Compact Ladder recommended

Most faithful compact training panel. Inputs stack on the left, Stage 1 channels appear as example tiles, and the TME block explicitly shows mask latent as Q tokens with residual-only output to ControlNet.
